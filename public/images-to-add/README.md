Recommended main-card ratio: 4:3 or a landscape photo at least 1200 px wide.

After adding an image, set its matching `image` value near the top of `src/App.jsx` to:

```js
image: "/images-to-add/your-file-name.jpg"
```

The placeholder will then automatically become your image.
